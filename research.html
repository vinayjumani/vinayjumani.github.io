TierMoE: Expert Placement for Batched MoE Decode
Aug – Oct 2026 · Under review, MLSys 2027

Systems study of expert placement for MoE inference under batched decode, built on routing traces and a Belady-oracle simulator.

  • Characterized expert demand on OLMoE-1B-7B, Qwen3-30B-A3B and DeepSeek-V2-Lite. Batching makes each step touch 51% of Qwen3 experts at B=16, so recency-based caches thrash: llama.cpp’s expert cache ran 1.5–3.0× slower than a layer split.
  • Designed a static per-expert placement in llama.cpp that keeps frequency-selected hot experts on the GPU and runs cold experts in place on the CPU (avoiding PCIe transfers) with a sparse cold-expert operator. Up to 1.34× end-to-end speedup at B=4 on Qwen3-30B-A3B (1.60× with a 50% GPU budget) and 1.15× at B=16.
  • Pre-registered evaluation (thread pinning, interleaved repeats) with correctness gates and a GSM8K check; reports where gains reverse (B=32, coarser MoEs).
QIRA-L: Training-Free Token Halting via Closed-Form K/V Transport
Dr. Amitava Das · Aug 2026 – present

Training-free intermediate-layer token-halting method that predicts later-layer keys and values with closed-form, layer-specific ridge transport, without modifying the LLM backbone.

  • Retained 89.1% of dense LongBench quality on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct at 89% of prompt tokens halted (+25.7 pts over the best drop baseline).
  • Measured 1.50× prefill speedup; retained 98.3% of dense quality on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct at the same budget.

EEG-LLM Cognitive Decoding
Undergraduate researcher · NTU Singapore & BITS Goa · Nov 2025 – present · code

Working on EEG-to-language decoding using large language models to study cognitive signal representations and neural–linguistic alignment.

  • Designing deep learning pipelines for EEG preprocessing, time–frequency transformations and multimodal embeddings.
  • Implementing and evaluating LLM-based architectures for intent / semantic prediction from biosignals (transformers, contrastive learning, calibration metrics).
  • Running experiments on benchmarking EEG datasets; tracking model accuracy, calibration (ECE) and robustness.
  • Fine-tuned the LaBraM Transformer in PyTorch, achieving 96.9% LOSO accuracy across 73 subjects, and built an attention-based interpretability framework using Grad-CAM with feature-level analysis of EEG representations.
  • Manuscript under review at a Q1 journal, with Dr. Yuvaraj Rajamanickam (NTU/NIE) and Dr. Amalin Prince (BITS Goa).
